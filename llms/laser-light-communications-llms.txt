# Laser Light Communications

> Laser Light Communications Inc. (Reston, Virginia; founded 2012; CEO Robert Brumley) is building a
> carrier-neutral "Multi-Domain Global Data Platform" that converges greenfield terrestrial and subsea
> optical fiber, cable landing stations, carrier-neutral data centers and edge facilities, and a planned
> 12-satellite medium-Earth-orbit free-space optical constellation branded HALO (All-Optical Hybrid
> Global Network). It sells wholesale capacity as Data Transport as a Service and Network on Demand to
> service providers, hyperscalers, CDNs, financial institutions, governments and large enterprises.
