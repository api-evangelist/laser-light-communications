Laser Light Holdings, Ltd. (“Laser Light”) today announced its Request for Information (RFI) process seeking potential prime contractors and associated subsystem/component suppliers for its planned High Articulation Laser Optics (HALO) satellite constellation. HALO will be a core component of Laser Light’s ultra-high-speed, on-demand global network spanning space, terrestrial, and subsea infrastructure and designed to meet growing worldwide demand for artificial intelligence (AI) connectivity.
